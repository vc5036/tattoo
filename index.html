Aftercare

Healing is part of the art

How you look after a new tattoo shapes how it settles. These are general pointers — your artist’s own instructions always come first.

  • Follow the cleaning and care instructions your tattoo artist gives you.
  • Keep the tattoo clean and handle it only with clean hands.
  • Don’t pick, peel or scratch the area as it heals.
  • Avoid unnecessary friction from tight clothing and avoid soaking it in water while healing.
  • If you notice signs of infection or anything concerning, contact a qualified healthcare professional.

This information is general and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Aftercare guide

This is a general guide. Always follow the specific instructions your artist gives you, as the right care can differ by tattoo, placement and skin.

The first days

  • Leave the covering your artist applied for as long as they advise.
  • Wash your hands before touching the tattoo.
  • Clean gently as instructed by your artist, then pat dry with a clean towel or paper towel.

While it heals

  • Apply any recommended aftercare product in a thin layer only.
  • Don’t pick, peel or scratch flaking skin or scabs.
  • Wear loose, clean clothing over the area to limit friction.
  • Avoid soaking (baths, pools, sea) until your artist says it’s healed.
  • Keep it out of direct sun, and protect healed tattoos with sunscreen.

When to seek help

Some redness and tenderness is common. If you notice spreading redness, increasing pain, swelling, unusual discharge, fever or anything else that worries you, contact a qualified healthcare professional promptly.

This guide is for general information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
